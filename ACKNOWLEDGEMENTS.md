## Upstream work

This project implements and extends *“Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own
Weights”* ([arXiv:2611.21885v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885v2)). It uses
[`Qwen/Qwen3.5-27B`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-27B), vLLM, PEFT,
safetensors, `math-verify`, Modal, and the upstream MATH-500/DeepScaleR
datasets. Their licenses and terms apply to those components.
